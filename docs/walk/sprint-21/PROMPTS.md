# Art board prompts (Sprint 21)

Make each board with a free image generator (Gemini, ChatGPT, Microsoft Designer / Bing Image Creator, Adobe
Firefly free credits). One square or tall image per theme. Send the image in the chat; it is cut locally and only the
drawings are committed (never the board).

Shared text (add the theme line below it):

> A sticker sheet of about 14 separate hand-drawn chalk-and-crayon illustrations, in the same style as a child's
> chalkboard drawing: thick textured chalk strokes, bright crayon colors, white chalk outlines. Each drawing stands
> alone with plenty of empty space around it, nothing overlapping, no text, no letters, no numbers, no frames. Plain
> flat solid dark navy background (#10203F), no gradient, no texture on the background.
